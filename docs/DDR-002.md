## Trust Boundary

the team's Phase 1 template files, and my own HW3–HW5 architecture documents went
to Anthropic as prompt material. No credentials, no teammate's private data,

**Reverse crossing: nothing.** The tool returned prose. No dependency, no
package, no configuration, no hidden instruction file. Unlike a hosted app
builder, a chat assistant drafting markdown brings nothing back into the
repository except the text I accepted.

## Findings

**What was delegated and what was not.** The tool wrote the criteria wording,
every sentence in the "Why" column, the notes on the scores, and the sensitivity
paragraph. **I made the five weight decisions and the fifteen score decisions**,
choosing from options it laid out and able to change any of them. That is a real
division, and it is also a generous one to me: the reasoning *as written* is
largely the tool's phrasing of judgements I agreed with rather than sentences I
composed.

**Could not fully verify, and what I did about it:**

1. **The claim that all five of us deployed a Worker and D1 in HW4 and HW5.** I
   did not check anyone's repositories before asserting it, and the related claim
   about team size was wrong until Prince caught it. The capability weight of 4
   rests on this, so it is the assumption most worth someone contradicting.
   *Disposition:* corrected the count; the per-member claim is still unverified
   and is flagged here rather than left implicit.

2. **The characterisation of the Buy option.** The Gate scores Buy a 2 on fit
   because "the nearest products show openings or courses, not what to learn
   next." I did not survey the market myself; I accepted that framing. If a
   product exists that answers the student's actual question, that score is wrong
   and the result changes. *Disposition:* said so in the PR description and asked
   explicitly to be contradicted. Nobody has yet.
